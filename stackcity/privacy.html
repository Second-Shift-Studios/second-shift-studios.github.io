Stack City Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026

The short version: Stack City does not collect, store, or share any of your personal information. Your town and progress are saved only on your own device.

Who we are

Stack City is made by Second Shift Studios ("we", "us"). If you have any questions about this policy, email us at SecondShiftStudiosLLC@gmail.com.

Information we collect

None. Stack City does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information, and it does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking tools.

Your game data

Your progress (coins, gems, buildings, decor, settings and similar game data) is saved in files on your device only. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Deleting the app deletes this data. If your device backs up apps through iCloud, Apple may include this data in your backup under Apple's own privacy policy.

Tracking

Stack City does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Beta testing (TestFlight)

If you test a pre-release version through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share limited information with us, such as your tester email address, crash reports, device model and iOS version, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. We use this only to fix bugs and improve the game, and we never sell or share it. TestFlight is covered by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Stack City is a family-friendly game. Because the game does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we add features in the future (such as optional ads or in-app purchases) that change how data is handled, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date before those features are released.

Contact

Questions or requests? Email SecondShiftStudiosLLC@gmail.com.
